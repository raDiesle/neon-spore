The same numbers the screen after the run draws, moving as the wave is played. Every one of them takes both players to move, and none can be read backwards to say who missed — that is the rule docs/spec/structure.md 7.2 puts on this screen, not a style choice.

THE SPEC, VERBATIM

Every file in docs/spec/, read off disk on load. The other tabs parse the design into entries, and a parse keeps what fits an entry — the naming rules, the categories, the names that were examined and rejected, the ceiling on how many silhouettes stay distinct over a voice channel are none of them a row with a badge. This is where the rest of it is.

Nothing here is editable. The spec is argued over in the repository; this is the copy you read while writing a wave.
