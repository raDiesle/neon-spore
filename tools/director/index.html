Every contour the project has, in three states. A DRAFT was drawn for a named idea in the store and offered to it. A FREE shape has nothing behind it — tuned in legacy/style-guide.html and never spent. TAKEN is what the game already draws, here so a proposal is judged beside what it will stand next to.

They animate, because whether a lobe swings or only shivers is most of what tells two blobs apart at 26 px. The sway is the same data the game draws with, measured in tiles, so a card shows the motion the field would show and not an impression of it.

A shape is not a name, and a suggestion is not a decision. Anything that takes one still gets its own name out of the bestiary's four naming rules, and the idea it was drawn for is free to refuse it.

The skin is the same question docs/alive.md sends to a vote: whether an interior survives at all, or whether the outline is the whole of what a player reads. LINE is the control — it is what these cards drew before there was a choice.

A base blob and a click-together list of parts, so a recipe can be tried before it is written down. Nothing here is saved — closing the tab or picking another view keeps the card only until the next reload — and nothing it draws reaches packages/content on its own. Build something worth keeping, then copy the recipe at the bottom into grown-bodies.ts or jelly-bodies.ts by hand.

Click a part under any of the five headings to attach it; each new one finds its own place on the rim rather than reflowing the ones already there. The six small buttons on each attachment nudge only that one — rotate, resize, mirror, remove.

What is left to do on a boss. Not the act order — that is the page this one replaced, and it listed the bosses the game has. This one lists a boss whose simulation landed and whose look nobody has written, then what every finished boss still owes, in the words its own section uses: a beat with no picture of its own, a call window the game cannot judge, a fight nobody has watched at tempo. It is the largest pool of decided, sized, unstarted work in the repository, and until now nothing drew it. The last group is the other file — the primitives a choreographed scene would need and has no shipped ancestor for. Which group a boss is in is the heading it stands under in docs/spec/bosses.md, so a look that lands moves it by being moved in the spec.
