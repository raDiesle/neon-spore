# LANCE — ideas for the beam

The owner asked on 18 October 2026 for a page of new enemies that need the
**LANCE** to kill. None of this is built, and nothing here is decided.

The lance today, in one breath: player 2 **holds** red or cyan, player 1
**keeps the cannon still**, and after three beats the whole column burns in
that colour. A rock or the other colour stops it. Today only two bosses need
it, THE GEODE and THE FLUE. No small enemy does.

Every idea had to pass three tests:

- **One sentence a player can say back.**
- **Nothing the player controls travels.**
- **Somebody has to talk.**

---

## New enemies

### THE TOTEM — it grows back faster than you shoot

> *"Don't tap it. Hold still, I'm charging."*

A tower of four bodies in one column, one colour. Every beat it grows one
back. Single shots can never catch up. One beam takes the whole tower.

- **Player 1:** stays under it for three beats.
- **Player 2:** holds the colour.

### THE SPLITTER — a shot makes it worse

> *"Leave it! Every shot makes two."*

A bolt splits it into two, one under the other. Two become four. The beam
kills the whole family at once, because they all stand in one column.

- **Player 1:** has to stop player 2 from tapping by habit.

### THE STRIPE — one wrong colour hides in the stack

> *"Third one's red. Shoot it first, then burn cyan."*

A column of cyan bodies with one red one in the middle. **Only player 1
sees the red one.** The beam stops at it, so it must go first, with a bolt.

- **Player 1:** calls the odd one out.
- **Player 2:** taps it, then holds.

### THE TIDE — the column changes colour

> *"It flips on four. Hold cyan now."*

The whole column flips red, cyan, red, every two beats. The beam goes off
three beats after the thumb goes down, so player 2 must hold the colour it
**will be**, not the colour it is.

- **Player 1:** counts the beat out loud.

### THE SKITTER — it steps aside from a bolt

> *"Bolts are too slow. Only the beam."*

It sees a bolt coming and steps one column over, then back. The beam is
instant, so it cannot step away from it. But it moves around, so player 1
must guess where it will be in three beats.

- **Player 1:** picks the column and holds it.
- **Player 2:** holds the colour.

### THE CRUST — a bolt bounces off

> *"That one's crusted. Charge it."*

A body with a hard skin. A bolt bounces off. A beam cracks it. **Only
player 1 sees which bodies wear a crust**, so player 2 shoots the rest and
waits to be told.
