# SUCK — ideas for the maw

The owner asked on 16 October 2026 for a page of ideas that need **SUCK**: new
enemies and mechanics that are won with the maw, and ways to make the two pod
brushes we have go further. The pods are used rarely. None of this is built,
and nothing here is decided.

What exists today, in one breath: a **pod** hangs in a column with a clock
round it. Player 2 shoots it loose (either colour), it falls and slides,
player 1 puts the cannon within two columns of it and presses SUCK as it
reaches the hull. If the clock runs out first, the wave is lost. A **hollow
pod** looks like a pod to player 1. Only player 2 can see it is hollow, and
swallowing one loses the wave.

Every idea below had to pass the same tests:

- **One sentence a player can say back.** If the rule needs a second
  sentence, it is not here.
- **Nothing the player controls travels.** The maw stays where the cannon is.
  Nothing reaches up the field.
- **Somebody has to talk.** Each idea gives one seat something the other seat
  cannot see, or needs two hands at once.
- **No cargo.** A pod gives nothing. The owner rejected the purge and the
  shield-giving pod on 16 October 2026, and no idea here brings a reward back
  in through another door.

## New enemies that are beaten with SUCK

### THE MORSEL — built

Built on 16 October 2026: THE MORSEL's block, right after THE SWINDLE and before
THE LANCE (`docs/spec/bestiary.md`). Its variant with a shell, which needs red and then
cyan before it goes limp, is not built.

### THE SEEDER — killing it drops a debt

Built on 16 October 2026: THE SEEDER's block, after THE MORSEL's and before THE
LANCE (`docs/spec/bestiary.md`). What is below is what it was asked to be.

> *"Shoot it and it leaves a pod behind. You owe that pod now."*

A creature that drops a hanging pod in its column when it dies, with the
usual clock round it. Killing it is easy. What it leaves behind is not: three
seeders shot in a hurry are three clocks running at once.

- **Player 2:** chooses *when* to kill each one, so that the clocks don't
  pile up.
- **Player 1:** catches the pods one at a time.
- **What they say:** "Hold fire on the left one, we've still got a pod up."
- **Why it is easy:** the player sees cause and effect on the same tile. The
  creature dies, and the pod is exactly where it was.

### THE GLUTTON — it eats your pods if you are slow

Built on 16 October 2026: THE GLUTTON's block, after THE LANCE's and before THE
CROSSING (`docs/spec/bestiary.md`). What is below is what it was asked to be.

> *"That thing is going for the pod. Get it first."*

A slow creature that walks down toward a hanging pod. If it reaches the pod,
it eats it and grows a size: harder to kill, and one lobe bigger. The pod is
gone, and so is the wave's debt for it, so letting it eat is not a loss. It is
a trade, a bigger enemy for less to catch.

- **Player 2:** decides whether to free the pod first or shoot the glutton
  first.
- **Player 1:** either catches the pod or holds the column for the bigger
  shot.
- **What they say:** "Free the pod now, he's two rows off it."
- **Why it is easy:** a race between two things on screen. Nobody needs to be
  told the rule twice.

### THE ROE — a string of pods that falls together

Built on 16 October 2026: a wave, THE ROE, at the end of THE HUSK's block, and
`roe` on a pod in the director's STRING row (`docs/spec/systems.md`). What is
below is what it was asked to be.

> *"One shot frees all three. Hold the maw open."*

Three pods strung in a row across one column, one above the other. A single
shot frees the whole string and they fall a beat apart. The 1100 ms maw takes
the first two on one press, and the third needs a second press.

- **Player 2:** one shot, at the right moment.
- **Player 1:** two presses in rhythm: "one … and three."
- **What they say:** "Releasing — three coming. Suck, and suck."
- **Why it is easy:** it is the pod they know, three times, and the rhythm is
  the lesson. It is also the first wave that tells a player the maw has a
  *length*.

### THE CLUTCH — the hollow one is among them

Built on 16 October 2026: a wave, THE CLUTCH, after THE ROE. It needed nothing
new: the husk's tell was already player 2's alone. What is below is what it was
asked to be.

> *"Four pods. One is hollow. Only I can see which."*

Four pods hang at once, and one of them is hollow. Player 2 sees which one.
Player 1 sees four real pods. Every real pod has to be swallowed, and the
hollow one must not be.

- **Player 2:** frees the real ones in an order and calls each one.
- **Player 1:** catches what he is told to, and nothing else.
- **What they say:** "Not the second from the left. That one's empty."
- **Why it is easy:** it is the hollow pod's whole idea at the size of a
  wave. One of these four is a lie.

## Ways the pods we have could go further

### The drift is called, not seen

When a pod is shot loose, **only player 2** sees the little arrow showing
which way it will slide. Player 1 sees the pod start to fall and nothing else.

- **What they say:** "Loose — it's going right!"
- **Why:** today both players see the pod slide, and nobody has to speak. One
  arrow on one screen turns the catch into a sentence, which is what this game
  is for.

### The clock on one screen

The ring round a pod is drawn **only on player 1's screen**. Player 2 shoots
pods loose without knowing how long each one has left.

- **What they say:** "Free the top one first, it's nearly out."
- **Why:** it gives the seat that catches a reason to direct the seat that
  shoots. Today the pod is always player 2's call.

### The coloured pod

Built on 16 October 2026: a wave, THE CAGE, at the end of THE LANCE's block, and an option on any pod in
the director (`docs/spec/systems.md`). What is below is what it was asked to be.

A pod tinted red or cyan that only a shot of that colour frees. The other
colour bounces off it.

- **What they say:** "That one's cyan. Load cyan."
- **Why:** it puts the pod back into the conversation every other creature
  already has, which is about colour, with no new rule to learn.

### The heavy pod

Built on 16 October 2026: a wave, THE ANCHOR, after THE CAGE, and an option on any pod in
the director (`docs/spec/systems.md`). What is below is what it was asked to be.

A pod that falls straight down with no slide, but takes **two presses** of
the maw, one beat apart, to swallow. The first press grips it, the second
takes it in.

- **What they say:** "Got it — again!"
- **Why:** a second shape of catch for player 1, so the maw is not always a
  single tap.

### The pod that swings

Built on 16 October 2026: a wave, THE PENDULUM, after THE ANCHOR, and an option on any pod in
the director (`docs/spec/systems.md`). What is below is what it was asked to be.

A pod that hangs on a thread and swings across three columns, like the
LANTERN look in VERSUS. Shooting it loose at the end of the swing sends it
off in that direction.

- **What they say:** "Wait for it to swing left — now."
- **Why:** player 2 times the release so that it lands where player 1
  already is. The cannon never has to chase it.

### Hollow pods that break loose on their own

A hollow pod that drops on its own at a random beat, with nobody shooting it,
so player 1 has to *not* press SUCK while it falls past. Player 2 sees it is
hollow, and player 1 sees a pod falling toward him.

- **What they say:** "Leave it! Don't suck."
- **Why:** the hollow pod today mostly asks player 2 not to shoot it. This
  makes it player 1's restraint, the half of the lie that is his.

## Waves that only use what already exists

Use these first, because they need nothing new built:

- **Two pods, one cannon.** Two pods freed a beat apart in columns three
  apart. The catch reaching two columns either side makes it possible, but
  only just.
- **Pod and hollow, side by side.** Two pods in neighbouring columns, one of
  them hollow. Player 1 has to take the real one *without* the hollow one
  landing inside his reach on the same press.
- **A pod at the end of a rock wave.** A pod freed while the last rocks fall,
  so player 1 chooses between the shield and the maw on the same beat.
