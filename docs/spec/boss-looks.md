# Boss looks waiting for a yes

Every boss below has a picture nobody has ever looked at on a real frame. The
work is decided and sized — `.claude/skills/new-boss` §6.3 says what a finished
one has to be — and **none of it may be started until the owner asks for that
boss by name.**

The owner, 20 September 2026: *"not every boss I like to keep or I need to
improve and fix the boss levels."* Twenty-four of these stood in `docs/queue.md`
where any lane could claim one, which meant a session could spend a sitting
making a boss beautiful the week before it was cut. They live here instead. A
list is not a licence; this page is a menu.

**To start one, say its name.** A lane then moves that boss's lines out of this
file and into `docs/queue.md` as a single item, claims it and works it the
ordinary way. When a look lands, its name comes off the list here.

## Its picture has never been looked at on a real frame

**Bosses:** PINBALL, THE SCOUT, THE PULSE, THE DIASTOLE, THE BATON, THE
UNDERTOW, THE THROAT, THE ORRERY, THE CANDLE, THE GORGE, THE CURTAIN, THE
TASTER, THE SINEW, THE LEDGER, THE SURGE, THE LEAD, THE SCUTTLE, THE ANTIPHON,
THE HIVE, THE CAIRN, THE WELL, THE SPLICE, THE REPRISE

Between four and thirteen files draw each of them, all named for it under
`packages/render/src`. What is there works and was written without ever being
seen: shapes that pass a test and have never been judged by an eye.

The state a boss has today is the state to draw. This is detail, not a picture
per state — a grown contour with lobes rather than an outline, a material that
reads as shell or plate or skin, and never a filled rectangle with a stroke
round it.

THE BATON carries one extra line: two of its states arrived on 18 September
2026 drawn by a session with no eye — a swelling socket is the husk grown half
again and shaking, and the two handle rings are the shipped ones. Both want the
same pass as the rest of the arm.

A picture is judged by an eye on a real frame, which a cloud session does not
have, so each of these is the owner's own machine — and it ends with one PNG
sent to him, never a description.

## SNAKE's board, as opposed to SNAKE

**Bosses:** SNAKE

The animal landed on 20 September 2026: a splined body with a neck and a taper,
scales, a lit back, a ground shadow, and a head of two hinged jaws. What was
split off and never started is everything that is not the animal — the things
standing on a tile to be shot and swallowed, the shot, the acid where it lands,
the crumple between two attempts, and the body coming out of the ship.

They share one question, which is why they are one item: a board of counters on
a grid, or a place.
