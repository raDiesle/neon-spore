## `4e577db` — the lance is on one panel now, not every panel

> on a wave that does not ask for it, is the lance button gone from player 1's panel?

> on the wave that does ask for it, does the panel read as its own set rather than as the usual one with a button added?

- **subject** the strip of controls along the bottom of the screen — player 1's half, the round buttons beside the cannon slider
- **changed** player 1 used to have three buttons on every single wave in the game: SHIELD, SUCK and LANCE. The lance is the slow one — you hold it down and the cannon fills up — and only one wave in the game is built around it, so on the other twenty-two it was a button sitting there with nothing to do. Each wave now comes with a whole named panel instead. Almost every wave gets the ordinary one, which has SHIELD and SUCK and no lance at all. The wave called THE LANCE (wave 21) gets a different panel, and that panel is not the ordinary one with the lance added — it has SHIELD and LANCE, and SUCK is gone from it, because the maw and the lance are the same opening on the ship and pressing SUCK empties whatever the lance was filling. A panel that is not the ordinary one prints its name in small letters at the top-left corner of the strip
- **decide** play any early wave and look at player 1's buttons: are there two of them, and is the lance really not there anywhere — including if you tap the empty space to the right of them? Then jump to wave 21 and look again: does that strip read as a different panel the wave handed you, or does it just look like the usual one with something swapped while you weren't watching?
- **before** three buttons for player 1 on every wave — SHIELD, SUCK, LANCE — whatever the wave was about
- **after** two buttons on an ordinary wave (SHIELD, SUCK); on wave 21, SHIELD and LANCE with SUCK gone and a small LANCE PANEL plate on the edge of the strip
- **where** `bun run preview`, then the game at the port it prints — the right-arrow key steps forward a wave at a time, so hold it to 21. Worth looking on a phone-shaped window in both the one-player-per-screen views and the both-halves-at-once view
