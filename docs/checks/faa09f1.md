## `faa09f1` — CILIA drawn without asking the browser

> does CILIA's fringe still hug the body exactly as it did, now that it is drawn without asking the browser where the contour is?

- **subject** CILIA, the skin that draws a body as a fringe of a hundred short strands at its edge, plus a few over its middle, all swaying and leaning together
- **changed** every strand used to find its own starting point by asking the browser to measure along the body's outline — a hundred separate measurements, every single frame, on every card that wore this skin. That was slow enough to matter once many cards were on screen at once. It now takes one cheaper reading of the outline a frame (64 points instead of a hundred) and works each strand's starting point out from that by simple math instead of asking the browser again and again
- **decide** put CILIA on a body and watch it move — does the fringe still sit tight against the edge, sway the same way, and lean the same way when the body moves, or does any strand look loose, drift off the body, or twitch differently than before?
- **before** CILIA on any body, from before this change — the SHAPES tab's git history has it, or compare against the feel of any other skin's edge-hugging
- **after** CILIA on the same bodies now
- **where** the director, NOT BUILT YET → SHAPES, SKIN → CILIA, watched over a few of the sixty cards, especially ones that move (own-motion on) so the lean is visible
