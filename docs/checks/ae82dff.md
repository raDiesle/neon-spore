## `ae82dff` — bodies cast shadows on each other

> does a body in another's shadow still read as the colour it is?

- **badge** implementation
- **subject** bodies shade each other, and the contact shadow leans along the key light
- **changed** a new cast-shadow pass darkens bodies below and right of a caster, and the hull's contact ellipse leans with the light instead of sitting straight underneath
- **decide** at tempo, does a shaded creature still name itself red or cyan, and does the leaning ellipse read as contact rather than as a second body?
- **before** every body was lit from nowhere by its neighbours, and the contact ellipse implied a lamp directly overhead
- **after** a soft darkening on the diagonal neighbour, and a contact shadow that slides right as a body falls and lands under its own foot
- **where** the game, any wave with two bodies a row apart; the moment a body arrives on the hull
