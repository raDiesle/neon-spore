# a78b808 — THE MAZE becomes a wheel the pilot turns and the navigator fires into

## pulling the string until a way in lights on a column — does it click into place and hold, or does the wheel feel like it slips past

The click range is 180 thousandths of a column: 3.8 times the 47 the rim can
cross in a single tick, so arithmetically no column can be turned past, and
under a fifth of a tile, so the mouth should read as standing on the column
rather than near it. One click costs between 0.17 and 0.7 of a beat depending
where on the rim the mouth is. Whether that reads as a detent or as slipping
is the thing a thumb has to decide, at phone size.

## when the shot takes the wrong way in, can you see where it stopped and why, or does it only fail to arrive

The drum is drawn closed — rings and mouths, no corridors — so the only thing
either player learns is the route a shot has already paid for. A failed route
stays lit dim with a red cap on the cell it stopped in, and it stays there for
the rest of the wheel. The question is whether that cap reads as a wall the
shot ran into, or merely as the place the drawing stopped.
