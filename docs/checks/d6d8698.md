## `d6d8698` — the guard button says when it goes quiet

> holding the guard too early, can you now tell it has lapsed before the rock arrives, rather than finding out when the ship takes the hit

- **badge** implementation
- **subject** the guard button, in the moment its press expires unused
- **changed** An armed guard and a lapsed one drew identically once the light went out; the button now fades briefly instead of snapping dark.
- **decide** Press guard early and hold it — once the window closes, does the button visibly change, or does it look like it was never pressed?
- **before** an expired press and a never-pressed guard, indistinguishable
- **after** a fading glow that marks the moment the window closes
- **where** the guard button, bottom of the field, player one's side
